## English

A listing on The CSR Journal dated 3 October 2026 says more than 20 people were injured when a passenger bus travelling from Kolkata to Ranchi overturned early on Saturday morning on National Highway-33 in Jharkhand. Only the opening lines were retrievable, so the exact location, cause and condition of the injured are not included.
